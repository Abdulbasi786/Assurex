What can I manage with AssureX?+

Product registrations, warranty information, supporting documents, claim submissions and repair history. Reviewers and administrators have additional tools for validation, analysis and decisions.

Does AI make the final decision?+

No. Model outputs provide decision support. Reviewers inspect the evidence, warranty rules and model results before recording an outcome.

What documents can I upload?+

You can attach purchase invoices, warranty cards, product images, fault evidence and repair or diagnostic reports. The upload form shows the supported formats and size limits.

Who can access my workspace?+

Access depends on your account role and configured permissions. Product owners manage their records; authorized reviewers and administrators use operational tools.

How do I get started?+

Create an account, register your product and add the supporting documents. If you already have an account, log in to continue from your dashboard.
